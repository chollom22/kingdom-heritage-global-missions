Privacy Policy

How information may be handled when you use this website.

1. Introduction

Kingdom Heritage Network Global Missions respects the privacy of visitors to this website. This Privacy Policy explains how information may be collected, used and protected when you visit or interact with this website.

By using this website, you acknowledge this Privacy Policy.

2. Information We May Collect

Depending on the features available on the website, information may include information that you voluntarily provide, such as:

  • Name or other identifying information.
  • Email address or contact information.
  • Information included in messages or enquiries.
  • Information voluntarily submitted through website forms.

We may also receive limited technical information automatically, such as browser type, device information, approximate usage information and pages visited.

3. How Information May Be Used

Information may be used for legitimate website and ministry-related purposes, including:

  • Responding to enquiries.
  • Providing information requested by visitors.
  • Operating and improving the website.
  • Maintaining website security.
  • Understanding website usage and performance.

4. Cookies and Similar Technologies

This website or third-party services used on the website may use cookies or similar technologies to support website functionality, security, analytics or advertising.

Visitors may be able to control cookies through their browser settings. Disabling certain cookies may affect some website functions.

5. Third-Party Services

The website may, now or in the future, use third-party services for functions such as hosting, analytics, communications, payments, media or advertising.

Where third-party services are used, those providers may process information according to their own privacy policies and terms.

6. Advertising

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are used on this website, advertising providers may use cookies or similar technologies to provide and measure advertisements.

Visitors should review the applicable advertising provider's privacy information for details about how advertising data is handled.

7. Information Security

Reasonable measures may be used to protect information submitted through the website. However, no method of transmitting or storing information online can be guaranteed to be completely secure.

8. Children's Privacy

This website is not intended to knowingly collect unnecessary personal information from children. Parents or guardians who believe that a child has submitted personal information may contact the ministry so that the matter can be reviewed.

9. External Links

This website may contain links to external websites. Kingdom Heritage Network Global Missions is not responsible for the privacy practices or content of external websites.

10. Your Privacy Choices

Depending on the information collected and applicable law, you may have rights concerning your personal information, including requesting access, correction or deletion of information that you have provided.

11. Changes to This Policy

This Privacy Policy may be updated when website features, services or legal requirements change. The updated version will be published on this page.

Important: This Privacy Policy should be updated whenever new services are added to the website, such as payment systems, contact forms, analytics, advertising, membership systems or other third-party integrations.

12. Contact

For privacy-related enquiries, please use the official contact information provided on the Contact page .
