Church History

The journey and development of the ministry.

Kingdom Heritage Network Global Missions is a Christian ministry established to advance the Gospel of Jesus Christ through the accurate teaching of God's Word, true worship, prayer, intercession and Christian service.

The ministry was founded with a vision to help believers understand the Scriptures, grow in their relationship with God and faithfully communicate the message of the Gospel.

Through teaching, worship, prayer meetings, evangelism, publications, media broadcasts and other ministry activities, Kingdom Heritage Network Global Missions seeks to serve the Church and make the Word of God accessible to people in different communities and nations.

Ministry History: This ministry was birthed out of hunger to see lives, destinies, territories families and cities transformed into the image of God. By this the ministry had strong prayers and Evangelism at it's inception at for months in the Year 2019 up to 2022. The ministry have had major challenges of venue because it has been evicted from one venue to another which has been one of the challenges of slow advancement.The ministry was born out of sincere Passion to see people turn to the Lord. Prayers were made for months on the ground upon coming to latya where he lived in Jos south plateau state. This ministry is standing on consistent intercessions for territories, families and destinies.

The ministry has had Missions to schools in Jos south plateau and had harvest of souls in hundreds. Amongst which are, TCNN, Zion High Academy, Faithful and True PIONEER. The ministry has partnered with different bodies on the field, amongst which is kingdom Invaders Missions in kebbi State Nigeria.

The ministry is a member of PENTECOSTAL FELLOWSHIP OF NIGERIA, JOS CHAPTER AND ALSO REGISTERED OFFICIALLY IN COOPERATE AFFAIRS COMMISSION.The people that attended our meetings grew but because they were threatened by leaders and members of their local assembly who could not bring change to their lives they withdrew. Kingdom Heritage Network Global Missions have very challenging experiences from physical attacks to spiritual confrontation and also eviction from time to time.

The founder

Information about the founder of the ministry.

Apostle Justice Adama

Founder / General Overseer

Founder's Educational Background

The Founder of Kingdom Heritage Network Global Missions has undertaken a journey of academic and professional development, building a foundation in education, religious studies, and human resource management.

1. Primary Education

1995 – 2005

The Founder attended Holy Child Model Nursery and Primary School, Otukpo, Benue State, Nigeria, where he successfully completed his primary education and obtained his First School Leaving Certificate.

2. Secondary Education

2005 – 2011

He attended Wesley High School, Otukpo, Benue State, Nigeria, and later Living Stone Academy, where he completed his secondary education and obtained his Secondary School Certificate.

3. University Education

2014 – 2018

The Founder proceeded to university education, where he studied Religious Studies and successfully obtained his First Degree in Religious Studies. His academic training provided a foundation for understanding religious values, leadership, spirituality, and service to humanity.

4. National Youth Service Corps (NYSC)

After completing his first degree, the Founder relocated to Plateau State, Nigeria, where he participated in the National Youth Service Corps (NYSC), contributing to national development and community service.

5. Postgraduate Diploma in Education (PGDE)

2021

In furtherance of his academic and professional development, he obtained a Postgraduate Diploma Certificate in Education from the National Teachers' Institute (NTI).

6. Professional Certificate in Human Resource Management

2020

The Founder also obtained a Professional Certificate in Human Resource Management, strengthening his knowledge of organizational administration, personnel management, leadership, and human resource development.

7. Master's Degree in Theology

The Founder further pursued a Master's Degree in Theology at Prosperous Bible Institute, Nigeria. This advanced theological training further enriched his understanding of biblical studies, Christian doctrines, ministry, spiritual leadership, and theological research.

8. Master's Degree in Christian Psychology

Currently Rounding Up

The Founder is currently rounding up his Master's Degree programme in Christian Psychology at Lampstand University, Jos, Plateau State, Nigeria. This academic pursuit reflects his commitment to understanding the relationship between Christian faith, human behaviour, emotional well-being, personal development, and the application of biblical principles to human life.

Commitment to Knowledge and Leadership

His educational journey reflects a commitment to lifelong learning, leadership development, spiritual growth, and the advancement of humanity through education, faith, and purposeful service.

Christian Journey and Spiritual Experiences

A Journey of Faith, Passion, Service and Spiritual Growth

1. Early Christian Foundation

The Founder was raised in a Christian family, where he was introduced to the Christian faith and values from an early age. However, for historical and spiritual records, he made a personal commitment to Jesus Christ in October 2005, during his first year of secondary school. From that moment, his passion for God and the things of the Kingdom continued to grow.

2. Baptism of the Holy Spirit

In 2010, the Founder experienced what he describes as the baptism of the Holy Spirit. Following this spiritual experience, he became increasingly conscious of God's presence and calling upon his life, and began to experience opportunities to serve God and minister to others.

3. Wonderful Experiences and Divine Connections

Throughout his Christian journey, the Founder has experienced remarkable moments and divine connections. One of such significant experiences was his encounter with people of like passion at Kogi State University, Anyigba. These relationships contributed to his spiritual growth, encouragement, fellowship, and deeper commitment to God's purpose.

4. Challenges, Betrayals and Life Lessons

His journey has not been without difficult moments. Along the way, he has encountered different forms of betrayal, disappointments, and challenging experiences. Nevertheless, these difficult seasons have formed part of his personal testimony, teaching him valuable lessons about faith, perseverance, discernment, patience, and dependence on God.

Faith Through Every Season

Through wonderful experiences and difficult seasons alike, the Founder continues to pursue his Christian faith with passion, trusting God and remaining committed to the advancement of His Kingdom and the service of humanity.
