About audio processing

This page uses FFmpeg WebAssembly inside your browser. Your selected audio files are processed locally and are not uploaded to an external processing server.

Voice filtering uses audio filters to reduce some low-frequency and high-frequency noise. Voice enhancement uses equalization and compression. These are not AI noise removal.

Large recordings, especially one-hour recordings, can still require substantial memory. Keep this page open while processing. Do not close Chrome or switch off your phone.
