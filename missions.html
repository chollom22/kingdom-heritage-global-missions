Missions

Equipping believers with missionary power and helping advance the objectives of Kingdom Heritage Network Global Missions.

Our Missionary Purpose

Missionary work is part of the stated objectives of Kingdom Heritage Network Global Missions. The organisation seeks to equip believers with missionary power and help generations understand and play their role in God's program.

🌍

Missionary Equipping

To equip believers with missionary power.

✝️

Faith

To get souls engrafted in the faith after salvation.

🙏

Intercession

To consistently intercede for territories in order to preserve the heritage of God over them.

🌱

Destiny

To establish great destinies and equip them for the move of God.

Generational Purpose

Helping Generations

One of the organisation's stated objectives is to help generations know and play their role in God's program.

Mission-Related Objectives

These are the relevant objectives stated in the organisation's constitution.

To consistently intercede for territories in order to preserve the heritage of God over them.
