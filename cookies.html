Cookie Policy

Information about cookies and similar technologies used on the Kingdom Heritage Network Global Missions website.

What Are Cookies?

Cookies are small text files that websites may place on a user's device when the website is visited.

They can help websites remember information, maintain certain functions and understand how visitors interact with a website.

How We Use Cookies

Kingdom Heritage Network Global Missions may use cookies or similar technologies where they are necessary for the operation, security, functionality or improvement of the website.

The specific cookies used may depend on the features and services available on the website at a particular time.

Types of Cookies

Essential Cookies

Some cookies may be necessary for certain website functions to operate correctly.

Preference Cookies

These may help remember certain choices or preferences made while using a website.

Analytics Cookies

If analytics services are used in the future, cookies may help us understand general website usage and improve the website.

Advertising Cookies

If advertising services such as Google AdSense are used, third-party technologies may be used in connection with advertising and measurement.

Third-Party Services

Some website features may be provided by third-party services. These services may use their own cookies or similar technologies according to their respective policies.

Examples may include analytics, advertising, embedded media, payment services or other external services that may be added to the website.

We recommend reviewing the privacy and cookie policies of relevant third-party services when using their features.

Google Advertising

If Google advertising services are enabled on this website, Google and its partners may use cookies or similar technologies to provide, measure or personalise advertising in accordance with their applicable policies and user settings.

Advertising features may change as the website develops.

Managing Cookies

Most modern web browsers allow users to manage cookies through their browser settings.

Depending on the browser, users may be able to block, delete or restrict cookies.

Please note: Disabling certain cookies may affect the functionality or performance of some websites or services.

Changes to This Cookie Policy

This Cookie Policy may be updated when new website features, services, analytics tools, advertising technologies or other technologies are introduced.

Users are encouraged to review this page periodically for the latest information.
