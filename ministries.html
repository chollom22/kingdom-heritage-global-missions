Our Ministries

Areas of ministry activity reflected in the objectives of Kingdom Heritage Network Global Missions.

Ministry Focus

The following areas are presented directly from the organisation's stated objectives.

✝️

Faith & Salvation

Helping souls become engrafted in the faith after salvation.

🌍

Missions

Equipping believers with missionary power and supporting the missionary purpose of the organisation.

🙏

Intercession

Consistently interceding for territories in order to preserve the heritage of God over them.

🌱

Destiny Establishment

Establishing great destinies and equipping them for the move of God.

📖

Believer Equipping

Equipping believers and helping generations know and play their role in God's program.

🤝

Kingdom Service

Undertaking activities that are incidental to the attainment of the organisation's objectives.

Our Constitutional Objectives

These objectives provide the foundation for the ministry focus presented on this website.

1

To get souls engrafted in the faith after salvation.

2

To equip believers with missionary power.

3

To help generations know and play their role in God's program.

4

To consistently intercede for territories in order to preserve the heritage of God over them.

5

To establish great destinies and equip them for the move of God.

6

To do any or all things deemed incidental to the attainment of the objectives of the organisation.

Note: The ministry areas displayed on this page are an organised presentation of the objectives contained in the organisation's constitution. They do not introduce additional programmes or claims about the organisation.
