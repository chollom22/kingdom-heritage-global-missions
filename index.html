Kingdom Heritage Network Global Missions

Kingdom Heritage Network Global Missions is an interdenominational ministry established as a not-for-profit and non-political organisation.


The organisation exists to pursue its stated objectives through ministry, missions, intercession, equipping believers and establishing destinies.

Our Objectives

The objectives below are based on the organisation's constitution.

1

To get souls engrafted in the faith after salvation.

2

To equip believers with missionary power.

3

To help generations know and play their role in God's program.

4

To consistently intercede for territories in order to preserve the heritage of God over them.

5

To establish great destinies and equip them for the move of God.

6

To do any or all things deemed incidental to the attainment of the objectives of the organisation.
